About me

      
I’m a student who wants to build a career in cybersecurity. I started with web development and networking classes, then got curious about how systems break and how to make them harder to break.

      
Right now I’m practicing on class projects: scanning a network and writing up what I found, building a system with Django, and testing how rate limiting helps stop denial-of-service attacks.

      
I’m still a beginner, and I’m learning something new with every project.
